Saurabh Palande

I’m a Robotics Software Engineer specializing in computer vision and GPU-accelerated perception systems. My work focuses on building and deploying real-time vision pipelines for industrial systems, where performance and reliability are critical particularly in applications like aircraft sanding. I have hands-on experience developing custom CUDA kernels, optimizing GPU workloads using profiling tools like Nsight Compute, and designing perception systems that integrate RGB-D cameras, LiDAR, and embedded platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson. My background spans mapping, localization, and sensor fusion, with a strong emphasis on translating algorithms into efficient, real-world systems. I’m especially interested in solving challenging problems at the intersection of robotics, perception, and high-performance computing.
